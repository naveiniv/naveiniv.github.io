01 — About

The person behind
the content.

I'm a Computer Science student at Queen's University specializing in security, with a minor in Economics. I chose to study CS because of my passion for creating — whether that's building my own website or shooting advertisements for companies. I started making content to share what that actually looks like day-to-day — the coursework, the projects, the wins and the grind.

My approach is simple: show the real process, not a highlight reel.

Community Collab

Lay Low x
naveini.

In 2025, I partnered with Lay Low Cafe in Kingston to launch my own drink on the menu — "Naveini's Matcha." I led the campaign from concept to execution: planning, stakeholder communication, and promoting it across my platforms, which drove 100,000+ views and strong student engagement.

Every purchase gave back — the campaign raised and donated $1,000+ to Sexual Assault Centre Kingston (SAC Kingston). It's one of the collabs I'm proudest of, because it turned something I love making into real support for my community.
